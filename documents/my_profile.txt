# Muassir Ismail Dalhat (Midian)

**Computer Science | Cybersecurity | Artificial Intelligence | Machine Learning | Aspiring AI Security Engineer**

His long-term career objective is to become an **AI Security Engineer**, specializing in the design and development of **secure, reliable, and scalable AI-powered cybersecurity solutions for Africa**. He is open to collaboration and opportunities involving **cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, machine learning, data science, security engineering, and emerging technologies**.
